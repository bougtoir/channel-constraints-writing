# TITLE_FINAL_DECISION

Decision: retain the current title —

"Encoded-unit granularity, not phonological channel size, sets the
scale of writing-system symbol inventories"

Audit result: throughout the revised manuscript, "sets the scale" /
"locates inventory scale" is used as an architectural description —
granularity determines how many encoded units demand signs — not as a
causal estimate from the observational design. The one residual causal
phrase in Results §4.1 ("driven by architecture") was replaced with
"associated with". JQL tolerates strong conceptual titles, and the
contrastive "not X" structure preserves the falsified-hypothesis
framing central to the paper.
