# JOURNAL_SELECTION_GATE — Phase 6

Criteria (from protocol): scope fit for quantitative/comparative
linguistics + writing systems; Q1 where realistic; prefer
APC-free/hybrid/platinum; reasonable turnaround for a
single-author quantitative paper.

## Candidates

| # | Journal | Fit | Rank/OA | APC | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Journal of Quantitative Linguistics** (T&F) | Excellent: quantitative typology, statistical modeling of language structure | Q2–Q1 linguistics; hybrid | none mandatory (hybrid OA optional) | Primary target: dependence-aware stats + simulation framing is their staple |
| 2 | **Language Dynamics and Change** (Brill) | Strong: cross-linguistic quantitative inference, evolution | Q2; hybrid | optional OA | Good for "decoupling/progressive" narrative |
| 3 | **Written Language & Literacy** (John Benjamins) | Excellent scope: writing systems comparative | specialist; subscription | none mandatory | Smaller audience but exact topical fit; editors may want more script detail |
| 4 | **Glottometrics** | Quantitative linguistics | platinum OA | **free** | Zero-APC option; niche reach |
| 5 | **Journal of Language Evolution** (OUP) | Channel/evolution framing fits | Q1–Q2; hybrid | optional | Slower, broader audience |
| 6 | **Lingua** (Elsevier) | General theoretical | Q2; hybrid | optional | Possible but less quantitative-typology focused |
| 7 | **PLOS ONE** | Broad | Q1–Q2; OA | ~$2.3k mandatory | Fallback if fit-based rejections |

## Decision

**Primary: Journal of Quantitative Linguistics** — the paper's core
contribution (dependence-aware comparative statistics + an explicit
null/null-pattern result + simulation bounds) matches its remit;
standard submission carries no mandatory APC.

Backup order: Language Dynamics and Change → Written Language &
Literacy → Glottometrics (zero-APC) → Journal of Language Evolution →
PLOS ONE.

Format for JQL: standard article, numbered sections, author-date or
numbered refs accepted; we prepare numbered references; tables/figures
inline in one docx + separate files per journal practice.
