# TITLE_DECISION

Current title kept:

"Encoded-unit granularity, not phonological channel size, sets the
scale of writing-system symbol inventories"

Alternatives considered:

1. "Encoded-unit granularity, rather than phonological inventory size,
   predicts the scale of writing-system symbol inventories" — 'predicts'
   is no less causal than 'sets' and is wordier.
2. "Writing-system symbol inventory scale tracks encoded-unit
   granularity rather than phonological inventory size" — accurate but
   buries the contrastive structure.

Reason for keeping: 'sets the scale' is architectural, not causal-mechanistic —
the manuscript consistently frames granularity as the variable that locates
inventory magnitude (demand-side assignment), not as a proximate cause; JQL
tolerates strong conceptual titles; the contrastive "not X" clause preserves
the falsified-hypothesis structure. Confirmed 2026-10-03.
