## Regression test

`tests/test_protected_numbers.py` (3 tests, all passing):

1. **Tokens are defined** — every `{TOKEN}` in MANUSCRIPT.md / COVER_LETTER.md exists in the registry.
2. **No protected literal in templates** — the registry display strings of every token actually used must not appear literally in MANUSCRIPT.md, COVER_LETTER.md, `build_manuscript_docx.py`, or `build_cover_letter_2p.py`. 3. **End-to-end** — the builder output contains no unresolved `{TOKEN}`.
